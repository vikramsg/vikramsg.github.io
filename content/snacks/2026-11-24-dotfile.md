Almost always I work on 2 machines. My local Mac and a remote VM.
I also keep a `dotfiles` repo to manage custom config, for eg. for `nvim`, `herdr` etc.
The problem always was making sure that both machines stayed in sync, symlinks stayed in sync etc.

Now, finally I know a better way. Or actually more realized the better way.
The better way is just use `herdr-core`, which has the `hc` CLI and understands the concept of a machine.
So the agent now manages syncs and verifications on both machines using the `hc` CLI.
The mental model switch was actually triggerd by the flag `hc machine`.
