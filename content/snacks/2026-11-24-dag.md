---
title: "A non-deterministic DAG"
date: 2026-11-24
---

I am starting to think LLMs are reliable enough that we can experiment with modeling DAG calls as just an LLM model making a decision on what the next step is. 
The next step just uses that decision. 
A DAG tool like Airflow becomes more for monitoring and less for making explicit decisions.

I do think we still have to treat this step as a software component.
Maybe evals replacing tests, typed inputs and typed outputs.
But the conflict is to find the balance between over-constraining vs under-constraining.
