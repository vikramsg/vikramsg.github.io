The software development lifecycle is starting to look very different to me.
I keep saying the same phrases to my agent - "Monitor CI", "Triage deployment"...
For personal projects, I am starting to add slash commands for committing and merging.
Which is funny, since when buttons like this were introduced in Cursor, I disliked it.

I guess my mental models were not developed enought to take it in my stride.
The learning for me is to actually observe how I am interacting with agents, and what I keep repeating.
