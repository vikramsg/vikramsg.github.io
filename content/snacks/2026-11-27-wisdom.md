title: "Wisdom"
date: 2026-11-27
---

An idea I am increasingly convinced is right is the following definition (not my idea but I can’t remember where I saw it)

`Knowledge` - How to do something
`Wisdom` - What to do

At this point every single frontier model is better than all of us humans at `Knowledge` (or maybe we can call it intelligence).
But they are really bad at figuring out `What` to do.
Which is exactly where the Decision space or the Design space is.
We can make infinite decisions but which one to make?

So in fact the best we can do right now is be able to actually `Understand` the decision space and the trade-offs.
And then decide. Because my current strong opinion is that it is going to be hard for models to get better at deciding `What to do`.
