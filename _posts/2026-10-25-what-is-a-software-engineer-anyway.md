I feel the fear and anxiety of layoffs is not letting us all slow down and think clearly about what is happening. 
I know I am in a privileged position since I am not being laid off but maybe that helps me think about this differently.

And my current belief is that yes AI is definitely going to create a lot of jobs. 
But only eventually. Right now we are going through the pain of the workplace not correctly being organized around AI. And Twitter comments. And the economy.
But there is so much that needs reconfiguring. 
The software engineer has been going through a transition for a long time. It used to be a very specialized, isolated role. 
But over time it has been gradually shifting to a Full Stack role with engineers expected to handle frontend, backend, data whatever. 
This was happening pre AI.

But with AI, the timeline of this transition has shrunk. 
The expectation that this would take a few years is gone since it is already realistic to become full stack right now. 
If you are good at any one of backend, frontend, data etc, then with AI one can be a good Full Stack Engineer. Period.
So now we are moving much faster to the transition that was probably going to take another decade. That of becoming a Product engineer. 

Where not only am I supposed to be good at Full Stack but I can also do Product Management. Figure out what the product requires. Figure out how to test my assumptions. Figure out how to talk to customers.
And the fact is currently, companies have far too many layers for that to happen cleanly. So they are trying to figure out what to do by either following everyone else and firing arbitrarily or actually doing it intentionally without communicating what needs to change.
In effect, what I am saying is to please accept that the job is changing. And the expectation is going to be that we become Full Stack Product Engineers.
However once this transition is complete which I think will take atleast a few years, maybe 5, then there will be a lot more jobs. Because a lot more Products are going to be built.
