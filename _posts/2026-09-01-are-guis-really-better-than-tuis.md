I did something via vibe coding, but I did it because I wanted to prove something about which I had a very strong opinion

I had a strong opinion that gois are most definitely better than toys.
I kept collecting that opinion as I worked with various apps and observed how I felt using each one.
Of course, this is a strange era to be thinking about this because probably terminal applications were not so much in vogue for at least 30 years. However, the last couple of years they have exploded

I have long been a serious user of terminal apps like `neovim` and `tmux` but have always had a sort of love-hate relationship. 
`neovim` in particular was always so much about config.
I would always come in with the seductive power of its speed and just how good it felt to use it.
But then I would start trying to use it seriously and then disover some edge case that needed long hours of research.
Couple of days later I would go back to VSCode.
I even tried things like `LazyVim` but there was always some edge case.
In reality, it was all edge cases unless all you did was use `nvim` as just a text editor.
Which is how I kept using it since I always used it for writing blogposts like the one now!
