I love doing side projects.
And most of them never actually finish.
But I am always ready to start a new one with the absolute certainty that I must absolutely work on this one and that I will finish it.
If I were to statistically track completion rates, I would probably not bother.
But clearly, the act of starting one is not a rational decision, but an emotional one.
The beauty of a clean slate seems doubly attractive after day in and day out dealing with a large codebase where the number of possible directions is highly constrained.
And so side-projects.

## Past

However, what I have realized and I am sure others have pointed out as well is that even if sideprojects don't get finished there's an important reason why did them.
The reason is simply brain rewiring.
