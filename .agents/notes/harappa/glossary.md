# Glossary

We'll add terms here only as we actually use them.

## The Big Split

- **Glottographic** — Marks that encode *spoken language* (specific words, sounds, or grammar). Example: English, Chinese, Egyptian hieroglyphs.
- **Semasiographic** — Marks that convey *meaning directly* without encoding a specific spoken language. Example: road signs (`⛔`), coats of arms, math symbols (`+`, `=`), emojis.
